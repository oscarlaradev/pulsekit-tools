Why Use Client-Side Browser Tools Instead of Cloud Converters?

Most online converters upload your private photos, documents, or Wi-Fi passwords to third-party servers. PulseKit Tools runs 100% inside your device's browser memory using modern Web APIs (HTML5 Canvas, Web Gamepad API, Web Audio API, and Web Crypto API). That means instant processing speed, zero bandwidth waste, and complete privacy on Windows, Mac, Android, and iOS.

1. WebP Image Compression

Converting heavy PNG or JPEG photos to WebP at 80% quality typically cuts file size by 70–90% while preserving crisp visual detail—ideal for faster websites, Shopify stores, and Discord/WhatsApp sharing.

2. Detecting Controller Stick Drift

A healthy PS5 DualSense or Xbox analog stick rests within a 0%–6% deadzone at center. If your idle offset stays above 8–10% or jitters without touch, your potentiometer or Hall-effect sensor needs calibration or cleaning.

3. Gaming Mouse Polling Rate (Hz)

Standard office mice report at 125Hz (every 8ms), whereas competitive esports mice report at 1000Hz (every 1ms) or higher. Use our Polling Pad to confirm your USB port and mouse firmware are running at full speed.
